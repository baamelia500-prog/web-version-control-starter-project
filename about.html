What they are

Digital art programs give artists tools to draw, paint, and create artwork on a computer. A program supplies the brushes, the layers, and the canvas. The tablet or mouse supplies the hand movement.

Why compare programs?

Different artists have different needs. Some people may want a free program, while others may want or need more advanced tools.

A free program is often enough to learn on. Paid programs add features such as animation timelines and printing support, which matter more once the work leaves the screen.
